KALI COMMAND AI — PUBLIC DEPLOYMENT

1. Deploy backend to a public HTTPS hosting provider.
2. Get a public HTTPS URL/domain.
3. Put the public URL into:
   backend/.env -> PUBLIC_BASE_URL
   backend/.env -> GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI
   frontend/config.js -> API_BASE_URL

4. In Google Cloud OAuth Client:
   Add:
   https://YOUR-DOMAIN.example/oauth2callback

5. Never expose:
   GEMINI_API_KEY
   GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET
   SESSION_SECRET

6. Test:
   /api/health
   /auth/youtube

7. Configure Privacy Policy and Terms URLs.

8. Complete Google verification if required.

9. Build and sign release APK.
